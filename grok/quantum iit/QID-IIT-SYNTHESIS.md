## Part 1: The Three Papers

### Paper 1: Zanardi, Tomka, Venuti (2018)
**Title:** "Towards Quantum Integrated Information Theory"  
**Citation:** arXiv:1806.01421v2  
**Institution:** USC Center for Quantum Information Science & Technology

#### Key Contributions:

1. **First formalization of Quantum IIT**
   - Extends Tononi's classical IIT to quantum systems
   - Replaces probability distributions with density matrices
   - Replaces Markov processes with CP-maps (completely positive, trace-preserving)

2. **Quantum Cause/Effect Repertoires**
   ```
   ρ^(e)(P|M) = Tr_{P'} U(Ψ_M ⊗ 1_{M'}/d^{|M'|})  (effect)
   ρ^(c)(P|M) = Tr_{P'} U*(Ψ_M ⊗ 1_{M'}/d^{|M'|})  (cause)
   ```
   Where:
   - M = mechanism (subset of network)
   - P = purview (what M affects/is affected by)
   - U = unitary evolution
   - U* = Hilbert-Schmidt dual

3. **Quantum Integrated Information**
   ```
   φ^(x)(P|M) = min_{partitions} D[ρ^(x)(P|M), ρ^(x)(P_1|M_1) ⊗ ρ^(x)(P_2|M_2)]
   ```
   Measures irreducibility of cause/effect structure

4. **Global Φ**
   ```
   Φ(U) = min_{partitions P} D(C(U), C(U_P))
   ```
   Where C(U) is the conceptual structure operator encoding all concepts

#### Critical Insights:

- **Entanglement can activate integration:** Even factorized maps can have Φ > 0 if state is entangled
- **Lieb-Robinson bounds:** φ obeys locality constraints for local Hamiltonians
- **Phase transitions:** Different "phases" from dis-integrated (Φ=0) to holistic (extensive log Φ)

#### Connection to QID:

**DIRECT PARALLEL:**
- Their "noising" operation (setting M' to maximally mixed) = our φ-zone concept
- Their trace distance D = our measurement of distinguishability
- Their conceptual structure = our entrainment patterns
