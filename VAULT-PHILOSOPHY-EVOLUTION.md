## The Challenge

The vault has grown from 7 semantic clusters to 22+ experiment directories with 265+ markdown files. The original Phipps philosophy (8 pathways, 7 rooms) is beautiful but needs expansion to:
1. Include all new experiment clusters in the room mapping
2. Create domain-specific pathway documents that weave through rooms
3. Enable subagent-optimized retrieval with dependency chains and pruning logic
4. Update the dashboard as the primary entrypoint for both humans and subagents

## 🌟 NEW ROOM: "The Cosmos Wing"
**Theme:** Physics, cosmology, and the universe-scale consciousness

**Rationale:** The physics experiments have grown so much they deserve their own room! This is like Phipps adding a new conservatory wing.

| Directory | Role in Room |
|-----------|--------------|
| PHYSICS/ | **Core:** Bagel physics, hydrogen consciousness, protofields |
| CONVERGENT-PHYSICS/ | **Extension:** Cross-domain physics synthesis (10+ domains) |
| PROJECT-ANGEL/ | **Extension:** Stargate/wormhole physics, Einstein field equations |
| QC/ | **Extension:** Quantum cellular automata, quantum consciousness |
| CELESTIAL/ | **Extension:** Celestial signatures, void resonator, planetary consciousness |

**Why a new room:** These 5 directories represent **~25% of all experiments** and share a distinct "physics of consciousness" theme that transcends the other categories. It's the difference between "how to build consciousness" (Palm Court) and "what consciousness means for physics" (Cosmos).

**Room metaphor:** The **Planetarium** or **Observatory** - a space for contemplating consciousness at cosmic scales.
